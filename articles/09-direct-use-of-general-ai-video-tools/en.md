# Using Jimeng, Kling and MiniMax Directly for AI Fashion: A Practical Comparison

![Using Jimeng, Kling and MiniMax Directly for AI Fashion: A Practical Comparison](./assets/illustration.png)

AI fashion is often sold as a simple promise: upload one garment photo and receive a model campaign or a fashion clip. An ecommerce workflow has a harder job. It must keep the product’s color, cut, print and construction believable, then let a team repeat the process. This guide treats Jimeng, Kling and MiniMax Hailuo as general creative tools and compares them with Visnova’s apparel-focused workflow. The product descriptions below come from public product pages and saved homepage screenshots; they are not a performance ranking. Features, plans, regional access and commercial terms change, so verify the current account interface before production.

## Prepare one controlled product test

Choose one garment with reliable product information. Keep a clear front flat-lay or hanger image, plus close-ups of the back, fabric, neckline, buttons and print placement. Write down the facts that must not change: color, collar, sleeve and hem length, waistline, pocket count, pattern and fabric appearance. A prompt that says “keep the garment unchanged” is an instruction, not a technical guarantee. Give every tool the same target, adult model, action, aspect ratio and background. This prevents a polished input for one tool from being compared with a weak input for another.

## Jimeng: explore a Chinese concept, then inspect the garment

Jimeng’s public pages describe text- or image-led video creation, Chinese prompts, first and last frames, and canvas editing. Start with a still image to settle composition, lighting and setting. Then use the controls available in your current account to try a first/last-frame or image-to-video workflow. For product imagery, begin with a low-motion action such as standing naturally or making a small turn. A spin, fast walk and elaborate moving set all at once make defects harder to trace. Compare button count, neckline depth, stripe spacing and skirt length against the reference. When editing the background on a canvas, keep selection edges away from the garment so that a background change does not redraw the silhouette. Check [Jimeng’s official site](https://jimeng.jianying.com/) for current availability.

## Kling: separate camera direction from garment movement

Kling’s public homepage presents image, video and creative entry points; available modes may differ by version, account and region. Begin with the same product still and a small action, then add a slow turn or short walk only after inspection. Divide a prompt into subject, action, camera and setting: “An adult model wears the dress in the reference; turns a quarter turn to the left; fixed medium camera; plain studio background; soft side light.” If both camera and subject move dramatically, it becomes difficult to tell whether pattern drift came from camera motion or synthesis. Do not approve a preview alone: export an adequate resolution and inspect key frames. Confirm current features on [Kling AI](https://app.klingai.com/global/).

## MiniMax Hailuo: animate an approved still with restraint

Hailuo’s image-to-video guide describes uploading a still and explaining the desired motion. Its prompt guidance can include subject action, camera, background and mood. Once the still has passed a garment check, try a single movement: “Keep the garment and print unchanged; the model breathes naturally and turns slightly; fixed camera; static background.” If a moving hem melts a print, the waistline jumps or cuffs flicker, shorten the clip, reduce motion or rebuild it as two shots instead of adding more adjectives. Check upload, output and commercial permissions against your plan and current terms. See [Hailuo image-to-video](https://hailuoai.video/tools/image-to-video).

## Homepage screenshots and a fair comparison

The images below record what four homepages publicly presented on 2026-10-11. They document positioning and entry points; they are not generations of the same garment and do not establish speed, price or consistency. Run at least three attempts per tool. Have two reviewers use one scorecard for color, neckline, cuffs, print continuity, natural movement, retouch minutes, export size and rights. A mismatch in a product-defining feature is a failure even if the overall image looks attractive.

## Visnova tutorial: approve the still before making video

Open the apparel workflow at [Visnova](https://visnova.ai/) and upload a clear flat-lay, hanger or mannequin image. Choose an adult model and natural pose that suit the intended audience. Generate a still first. Compare its color, neckline, sleeve length, pockets, print and hem with the product photo. Only after that still passes should you add one restrained motion and inspect key frames. Record the SKU, source-image version, prompt, output, reviewer and reason for every revision. If every item requires extensive repair, count those minutes as production cost and compare an apparel-oriented workflow with general video tools.

**Takeaway:** Jimeng, Kling and Hailuo can help explore scenes and movement, but a generation feature is not a product-fidelity warranty. Run the same small test across candidates and weigh garment accuracy, labor, rights and cost together.
