# Jimeng, Kling, MiniMax, or Visnova? A Practical Review Framework for AI Fashion

> Long-form blog · competitor survey and practical review

![Jimeng, Kling, MiniMax, or Visnova? A Practical Review Framework for AI Fashion](./assets/illustration.png)

## Separate “looks good” from “matches the product”

AI video demos often emphasize dramatic motion. Fashion commerce has a different test: do the color, neckline, sleeve length, print placement, and silhouette still match the physical garment? Does the hem or pattern drift after a still becomes a clip? A tool that is excellent for a concept film may be a poor fit for producing dozens of product-page assets. This is a survey of features publicly described on official pages and the homepage screenshots saved in this library. Marketing statements are not independent benchmark results, and this article does not claim one platform has the “highest fidelity.”

## What each product publicly presents

**Jimeng** describes text-to-video and image-to-video, first- and last-frame inputs, Chinese-language prompting, and an intelligent canvas with functions such as local redraw, expansion, erase, and cutout. It is a useful candidate for Chinese concept exploration, setting changes, and combining images. A seller still needs to check whether a specific button, print location, or fabric texture survives. See the [Jimeng homepage](https://jimeng.jianying.com/).

**Kling**’s global homepage lists image, video, creative, shorts, and generation entry points, and its offering changes over time. A fashion team can include it when testing motion, camera mood, and animation from a reference image. The relevant question is whether multiple generations of the same SKU retain its neckline, pockets, hem length, and color—not whether one sample looks cinematic. See [Kling AI](https://app.klingai.com/global/).

**MiniMax Hailuo** offers text-to-video and image-to-video paths. Its image-to-video guide describes uploading an image and describing the desired motion; example prompt ingredients include camera, subject action, background, and mood. Model availability, ratios, and commercial permissions depend on the current account and terms. It can add gentle movement to an approved still, but motion alone does not prove that the garment is accurate. See [Hailuo image-to-video](https://hailuoai.video/tools/image-to-video).

**Visnova** presents a garment-focused path: start from a flat-lay, hanging, or mannequin image, choose a model and motion direction, and create fashion visuals. Its homepage also describes quick single-item creation and node-based batch workflows. That is Visnova’s stated product positioning; whether it fits your work must be checked against real SKUs. See [Visnova](https://visnova.ai/).

## Benchmark fairly with one real garment

Choose a garment with clear construction and stable color. Photograph front, back, and a fabric detail. Give every platform the same target: “Keep the original neckline, sleeve length, waist seam, color, and print placement. An adult model makes one slow turn in a plain studio; keep the camera fixed.” Use each product’s supported input path: test Chinese prompting and first/last frames in Jimeng; try low-motion image-to-video with the same still in Kling and Hailuo; use Visnova’s apparel flow for product-to-model content. Do not give one competitor a specially chosen reference and another a weak one.

Generate three outputs per platform rather than selecting a single lucky result. Have two reviewers score the same rubric: color and silhouette, construction details, print continuity, temporal stability, human correction minutes, usable channel formats, actual account cost, terms, and asset rights. Define rejection rules first—for example, a key product feature disappears or an unlisted trim is invented. Count manual repair in the cost; otherwise a “cheap” generation can hide expensive labor.

## A Visnova walkthrough: begin with the still

Open [Visnova](https://visnova.ai/), select one garment with complete product information, and upload a clean flat-lay, hanging, or mannequin image. Choose an adult model and a restrained setting that fit your brand. First create a still, then zoom in on the neckline, cuffs, waist, buttons, print, and garment outline. If it fails, improve the reference or regenerate; do not scale a flawed result to a batch. After the still passes, add one motion and inspect the clip frame by frame for a drifting hem or print. Interface names, output specifications, and plan rights may change; use the current account screen.

For concept mood, imaginative styling, or Chinese-scene exploration, Jimeng, Kling, or Hailuo may belong on the shortlist. If the core job is turning real SKUs into repeatable apparel product visuals, include Visnova and other fashion-specific tools in the same SKU test. Try the task first, then select based on evidence.
